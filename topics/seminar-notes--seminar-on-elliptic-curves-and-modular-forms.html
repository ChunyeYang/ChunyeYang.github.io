Seminar on Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms

This will be the notes for the Seminar on Elliptic Curves and Modular Forms during the fall semester of 2026 at the University of Maryland. This seminar is an introductory level treatment on the topics of elliptic curves and modular forms, following Neal Koblitz's book Introduction to Modular Forms and elliptic curves.
